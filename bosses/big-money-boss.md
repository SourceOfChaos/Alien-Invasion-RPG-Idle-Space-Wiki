## Big Money Boss

The Big Money Boss is the strongest boss encountered in the Winter Zone.

## Unlock Requirements

- Reach Level 15 (exact Level still needs to be confirmed)

## Description

The Big Money Boss wears a secret golden key around his neck. Without sufficient upgrades from James’ Shop, defeating him is impossible. Description taken from the Winter Bosses Letter gained on reaching Level 10.

## Respawn

- Respawn time: 6 hours

## Drops

- Secret Key  
- Base drop: 1 Secret Key per defeat

Secret Key drops are affected by:

- Double Resources upgrade from the Evolution Shop  
- Resources +X% upgrade purchased with Crystals in the Main Shop

## Usage of Secret Keys

Secret Keys are used to upgrade:

- Secret Mine  
  - Production  
  - Storage Capacity
