In **Alien Invasion RPG Idle Space**, players encounter multiple boss monsters throughout the game.  
Bosses act as **hard progression gates** and must be defeated to unlock further areas, quests, or mechanics.

Some bosses are **one-time quest bosses**, while others are **area bosses** that guard specific regions.

This page provides an overview of all known bosses and explains the core mechanics behind boss fights.
  
---

## Boss Types

### Quest Bosses
- Appear only once as part of a quest.
- Defeating them is mandatory to progress.
- Example: **Astronaut**

### Area Bosses
- Guard specific regions of the map.
- Must be defeated to unlock or fully access the area.
- Examples: **Robot**, **Mammoth**, **Big Money Boss**

All bosses function as **hard gates** — there are no optional or avoidable boss fights.

---

## Boss Mechanics

### Fixed Stats
- Bosses have a **fixed HP pool**.
- Boss stats do **not scale dynamically** with player progression.
- Damage dealt by bosses **before the Winter Region** is constant.

---

### Damage Scaling (Winter Region and beyond)

Starting with the **Winter Region**, boss fights introduce a special mechanic:

- Each successful hit the boss lands during an **uninterrupted fight** increases the damage of subsequent hits.
- The longer the fight continues without interruption, the more dangerous the boss becomes.

This makes extended boss fights increasingly risky.

---

### Fight Reset Conditions

If the boss leaves your **capture radius**, the following happens almost immediately:

- Boss HP resets to **~100%**
- Damage scaling against the player is **fully reset**

This effectively resets the entire fight.

---

### Design Intent (Interpretation)

The increasing damage mechanic was likely introduced to counteract excessive use of the **Home Base Healing Trick**, which allows players to survive long boss fights through repeated healing.

---

## Home Base Healing Trick

Bosses are **not bound to a fixed location** and will follow the player across the entire map.

This allows players to:

1. Lure a boss to any Home Base  
2. Capture smaller enemies nearby  
3. Trigger the Home Base full-heal mechanic  
4. Sustain the boss fight over an extended grind

As long as the boss damage does not scale high enough to cause a one-hit kill, this method allows bosses to be defeated much earlier than likely intended.

This strategy is especially effective against **early bosses without damage scaling**.
