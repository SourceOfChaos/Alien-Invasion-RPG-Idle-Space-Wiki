# Premium Shop

The **Premium Shop** can be accessed at any time via the **shopping cart icon** on the game screen, regardless of the player’s current location.

It currently consists of **four pages**:

- **Main**
- **DNA**
- **Winter**
- **Laboratory**

Additional pages unlock and expand automatically as the player progresses through the game.

---

## Main Page

The **Main** page contains various real-money offers priced in the player’s local currency.
It also features three permanent Crystal-based upgrades under **Power Up**.

### Power Up Upgrades

- **Bonus EXP from Humans**
- **Bonus Attack Power**
- **Bonus Resources from Humans**

All upgrades:
- Have **10 levels**
- Start at **Level 0** with **0% bonus**
- Have identical Crystal costs

## DNA & Winter Pages

These pages unlock with progression and are mechanically identical.

For each regional currency:

- A **free reward** is claimable every **8 hours**
- Two Crystal bundles are available:
  - **100 Crystals**
  - **500 Crystals**
- All rewards scale with:
  - drop bonuses
  - Best currently farmable enemy

The **100 Crystal bundle always equals 1/6 of the 500 Crystal bundle**.

---

## Example: Winter Gem 2 (WG2)

### Effective Drop Calculation

Base enemy value: **30,000**

Multipliers applied:

- James’ Shop WG2 bonus: **×160,001**
- Premium Shop maxed: **×2.00**
- Mutagen Market WG1–3: **×1.24**
- Vend-O-Mat WG1–3: **×1.02**

30,000 × 160,001 × 2 × 1.24 × 1.02 ≈ 12.1B WG2 per kill

Spawn rate: **1 enemy / 12 seconds**
⇒ **300 kills/hour**

12.1B × 300 = 3.63T WG2/hour

### 500 Crystal Pack
- Reward: **35.3T WG2**

35.3T / 3.63T ≈ 9.7 hours

### 100 Crystal Pack

9.7h / 6 ≈ 1.6 hours

## Double Resource Chance Consideration

At max level, the **Evolution Shop Double Resource Chance = 100%**, effectively doubling all farmed resources.

If this bonus is **not included** in Crystal pack calculations:

- Effective farming output doubles
- Crystal packs lose **~50% of their time value** after unlocking **Evolution Shop Double Resource Chance**

| Scenario | 500 Crystal Pack Value |
|--------|-----------------------:|
| Double Resource excluded | ~9.7 hours |
| Double Resource included  | ~4.9 hours |

The Conclusion is this makes Crystal packs primarily a **time-skip mechanic**.

- The **250 Crystal bundle provides ~3×** the resources of the 100 Crystal bundle
- Some resources have **no free option**
